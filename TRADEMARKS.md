# Name and icon

"Ateliera" and the Ateliera icon (the constellation A) identify this app and its maker, Anna McPhee. They are not covered by the MIT licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE) or by any other licence in this repository.

- You may say that your project is based on Ateliera, or that it works with Ateliera vaults.
- A fork or derived app must use a different name and a different icon, in the app, its store listing and its repository.
- Do not publish anything under the Ateliera name or icon in an app store.
