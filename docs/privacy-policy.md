# Ateliera privacy policy

Last updated: DRAFT

Ateliera is a private journal. What you write in it is yours, and it stays with you. This policy explains what the app does with your information, in plain words.

## The short version

- We don't collect anything. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The one exception is anonymous crash reports, which Apple passes on only if you have chosen to share them (see below).
- Your journal is a folder of files on your device, or in your own iCloud Drive if you choose.
- Your cycle is read from Apple Health on your device and is never saved by Ateliera.
- You can export everything and delete everything at any time.

## Who "we" are

Ateliera is made by Anna McPhee. "We" in this policy means the maker of the app.

## What we collect

Nothing. Ateliera has no account and no server, and it contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking code. We never see what you write, and we never receive any information about you or how you use the app.

If you choose to share crash reports with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports through App Store Connect. You can turn this off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

## What the app keeps on your device

- **Your journal.** Entries, reflections, dreams, gratitude, intentions, possibilities and any images you add are saved as files in a folder you choose, called your vault. Earlier versions of each entry are kept in the vault for 30 days, and deleted entries stay in its trash until you empty it.
- **Your rhythm details.** If you enter your birth date, time and place, or a home place, the app keeps them on your device to work out your chart and seasons.
- **Your settings**, such as theme and which rooms are shown.
- **Unsaved drafts**, so you don't lose writing if the app closes. They are removed when you save or discard the entry.

On iPhone and iPad, the app asks the system to keep the files it writes unreadable while your device is locked. How a vault folder is protected also depends on where you keep it: a vault in iCloud Drive is covered by Apple's iCloud security as well.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, Ateliera reads your menstrual flow records from Apple Health to show your cycle day and phase. It reads them on your device, works out the cycle in memory, and never saves it, sends it anywhere or writes anything to Health. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Ateliera.

Cycle days and phases are estimates. They are not medical advice, and they must never be used for contraception.

## Location

If you allow it, Ateliera uses your location to know which hemisphere you live in, so the seasons are right. When you choose a birthplace or home place, or let the app find yours, the place name or your coordinates are sent to Apple's location service to look up the place. Apple handles this under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We don't receive it. You can turn location off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Ateliera and type a place instead.

## Apple Intelligence

On devices with Apple Intelligence, Ateliera can word each micro-season for where you live. This runs entirely on your device. It is given the season, the moon and your home place, never your journal, your cycle or your birth details. Its words are shown as the season's description and are never added to your entries. You can turn it off in the app's Settings.

## iCloud Drive

If you keep your vault in iCloud Drive, Apple stores and syncs it to your own iCloud account, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We have no access to it. Health data is never written to your vault, so it never reaches iCloud through Ateliera.

## Widgets and Siri

The widget and the "Today's rhythm" shortcut show the season, the micro-season and the moon. They never show your entries or your cycle.

## Locking the app

You can lock Ateliera with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. While the lock is on, the app switcher hides what's on screen.

## Export and delete

- **Export:** Settings > Export a backup makes a ZIP of your whole vault and settings, in open formats (Markdown, JSON and your original images). You choose where it goes.
- **Delete:** Settings > Delete everything removes every entry, earlier version, deleted entry, setting and draft from the app. Copies you have made yourself, such as an iCloud Drive vault on another device or an exported backup, are yours to delete.
- **Deleting the app** also removes everything it keeps on that device. A vault in iCloud Drive or another folder you chose stays there until you delete it.

## Children

Ateliera is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly hold any information about anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling your information differently, this policy will change first, and the new date will show at the top. The full history of this policy is public at https://github.com/hello-nova-claw/ateliera/commits/main/docs/privacy-policy.md.

## Questions

Ask through [the project's issues](https://github.com/hello-nova-claw/ateliera/issues). Never include anything from your journal or your health records. To report a privacy problem privately, use [the security advisory form](https://github.com/hello-nova-claw/ateliera/security/advisories/new).
